COMPANY HR POLICY HANDBOOK
Acme Corp - Employee Handbook (Demo Document)

1. LEAVE POLICY
All full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance.
Unused annual leave can be carried forward to the next year, up to a maximum of 5 days.
Sick leave is separate from annual leave, and employees get 10 paid sick days per year.
A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days.

2. WORK FROM HOME POLICY
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week, subject to manager approval.
Fully remote work arrangements require written approval from the department head.
Employees working from home must be reachable during core hours: 10 AM to 4 PM.

3. PROBATION PERIOD
All new employees undergo a probation period of 3 months from their date of joining.
During probation, employees are not eligible for paid leave, but may take unpaid leave
in case of emergencies, subject to manager approval.
Performance is reviewed at the end of the probation period to confirm employment.

4. NOTICE PERIOD
Employees who wish to resign must serve a notice period of 30 days.
During the probation period, the notice period is reduced to 15 days.
The company may waive the notice period at its discretion, with full and final settlement
processed within 45 days of the last working day.

5. REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
Employees can claim reimbursement for approved business expenses such as travel,
client meals, and internet bills used for official work.
All reimbursement claims must be submitted with valid bills within 30 days of the expense.
Claims are processed within 10 working days after approval from the reporting manager.

6. CODE OF CONDUCT
Employees are expected to maintain professionalism and respect in the workplace.
Harassment, discrimination, or any form of workplace misconduct will not be tolerated
and may result in disciplinary action, including termination.
All employees must complete an annual Code of Conduct training.

7. HOLIDAYS
The company observes 12 public holidays every year, as per the official holiday calendar
published by HR at the start of each year.
Employees working on a public holiday are eligible for compensatory leave.

8. EXIT POLICY
Upon resignation or termination, employees must complete a clearance process involving
IT, Finance, and HR departments before their last working day.
Full and final settlement, including any pending reimbursements and leave encashment,
is processed within 45 days of the last working day.

9. MATERNITY, PATERNITY AND PARENTAL LEAVE
Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of fully paid maternity leave for up to two surviving children.
Maternity leave may be initiated up to 8 weeks prior to the expected delivery date.
An additional 4 weeks of leave may be granted in case of medical complications certified by a registered medical practitioner.
Male employees are entitled to 2 weeks (10 working days) of paid paternity leave, which must be availed within 6 months of childbirth.
Employees legally adopting a child under 1 year of age are entitled to 12 weeks of paid adoption leave.

10. BEREAVEMENT AND COMPASSIONATE LEAVE
Employees are eligible for up to 5 consecutive working days of paid bereavement leave upon the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, or sibling).
Up to 2 days of paid compassionate leave are provided for extended family members (grandparents or parents-in-law).
Additional unpaid time off may be requested through the reporting manager if travel is required.

11. HEALTH INSURANCE AND MEDICAL BENEFITS
All full-time employees are covered under the company group health insurance policy from day one of employment.
The standard policy covers hospitalization expenses up to $50,000 (or equivalent local currency) per family unit (employee, spouse, and up to two dependent children).
The company provides an annual health check-up allowance of up to $200 per employee.
Employees also have free, 24/7 access to the Employee Assistance Program (EAP) for confidential mental health and counseling support.

12. WORKING HOURS, ATTENDANCE AND OVERTIME
The standard working schedule is 40 hours per week, Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, including a 1-hour lunch break.
Core collaboration hours during which all team members must be available online or in person are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM.
Overtime for eligible non-exempt roles requires prior written approval from the department head and is compensated at 1.5 times the regular hourly wage or as compensatory off days.

13. LEARNING, DEVELOPMENT AND TUITION REIMBURSEMENT
The company encourages continuous education and allocates an annual professional development budget of up to $1,000 per employee.
Eligible expenses include technical certifications, professional conferences, online course subscriptions, and industry workshops.
Requests must be submitted to HR with manager endorsement at least 14 days prior to course registration.
Employees who leave the company within 6 months of completing a sponsored certification may be required to refund the certification fees.

14. PERFORMANCE APPRAISAL AND PROMOTION POLICY
Formal performance appraisals occur twice a year: a mid-year check-in in June and an annual evaluation in December.
Performance is evaluated on a 5-point scale (1 - Unsatisfactory, 2 - Needs Improvement, 3 - Meets Expectations, 4 - Exceeds Expectations, 5 - Outstanding).
Employees who receive a rating of 2 or below will be placed on a 60-day Performance Improvement Plan (PIP).
Promotions and salary revisions become effective on April 1st following the annual review cycle.

15. DRESS CODE AND WORKPLACE ATTIRE
The workplace follows a smart-casual dress code from Monday through Thursday.
Casual attire (such as clean jeans and casual shirts) is permitted on Fridays. Ripped clothing, athletic gym wear, beach sandals, and offensive graphic shirts are not permitted at any time.
Business formal attire is mandatory when meeting clients, external stakeholders, or attending official board meetings.

16. IT ASSETS, DATA PRIVACY AND CYBERSECURITY
Laptops, peripherals, and software licenses provided by Acme Corp are company property and must be used solely for legitimate business purposes.
Employees must enable multi-factor authentication (MFA) on all company accounts and lock their workstations whenever leaving their desks.
Installing unauthorized or pirated software is strictly prohibited and subject to immediate disciplinary inquiry.
Loss or theft of any company IT device must be reported to the IT Helpdesk and Security Officer within 2 hours of discovery.

17. ANTI-HARASSMENT, POSH AND GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL
Acme Corp enforces a zero-tolerance policy towards sexual harassment, discrimination based on gender, race, religion, or sexual orientation, and workplace bullying.
An Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) oversees all POSH (Prevention of Sexual Harassment) complaints.
Any grievance submitted to the committee is reviewed with strict confidentiality, and inquiry proceedings must conclude within 90 days of filing.
Retaliation against any employee who files a good-faith complaint or assists in an inquiry is strictly prohibited and results in immediate dismissal.

18. MOONLIGHTING AND CONFLICT OF INTEREST
Full-time employees must devote their complete professional attention to Acme Corp during business hours and may not engage in secondary commercial employment or freelance work for competing entities.
Passive personal investments and non-profit volunteer activities outside working hours are permitted, provided they do not interfere with job responsibilities or present a conflict of interest.
Any external consulting, board directorship, or intellectual property creation requires prior written disclosure and written sign-off from the HR department and Legal team.

19. BUSINESS TRAVEL AND PER DIEM POLICY
All domestic and international business travel must be pre-approved by the department head and booked through the corporate travel desk.
Flight travel is permitted in economy class for all domestic trips and flights under 8 hours duration.
Employees traveling for outstation business receive a daily meals allowance (per diem) of up to $75 per day.
Incidental expenses such as airport taxis and business internet can be claimed with itemized receipts under the standard reimbursement policy.

20. EMPLOYEE REFERRAL BONUS PROGRAM
Employees who refer candidates who are subsequently hired and successfully complete their 3-month probation period receive an employee referral bonus.
Referral bonus amounts are: $1,000 for junior to mid-level roles, and $2,500 for senior or specialized technical roles.
Referral bonuses are disbursed in the payroll cycle immediately following the new hire's probation completion date.
Members of the Talent Acquisition team and hiring managers for their own open positions are not eligible for the referral bonus.
